Important Disclosures

The disclosures below highlight risks and considerations specific to Zoey Wallet's stablecoin-native investing platform: investment risk, stablecoin and conversion risk, and the role of Zoey Wallet's third-party brokerage partner, alongside the general risks of investing.

03.01

General Disclosure

The following disclosures are provided to help you understand important risks and considerations associated with Zoey Wallet and the investment services accessible through it. They are not exhaustive and do not constitute legal, tax or investment advice.

03.02

Investment Risk

Investing involves risk, including the possible loss of the principal invested. The value of investments can go down as well as up, and past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. You should only invest amounts you can afford to lose.

03.03

Fractional Shares

Fractional shares may have restrictions on transfer and trading that do not apply to whole shares, including limitations on transferring fractional positions to an external brokerage account. These restrictions are determined by our brokerage partner and the relevant exchanges.

03.04

Market Volatility

Global equity markets can be volatile, and prices can change rapidly in response to economic, political or company-specific developments. Market volatility can affect the price at which your orders are executed.

03.05

Stablecoin Risk

Funding your account using stablecoins such as USDT or USDC carries risk in addition to ordinary investment risk, including the risk that a stablecoin may deviate from its intended peg to the underlying reference currency, and risks associated with the issuer, custody and redemption of the stablecoin itself.

03.06

Currency and Conversion Risk

Where funding involves converting stablecoins or other currencies, the rate and timing of conversion may affect the amount ultimately available for investment. Conversion terms will be disclosed to you at the time of the transaction.

03.07

Brokerage Services

Brokerage, execution and custody services made available through Zoey Wallet are provided by a third-party, independently regulated broker-dealer, and not by Zoey Wallet. Zoey Wallet is a technology platform and does not itself hold or custody customer securities.

03.08

Market Data

Market data displayed on Zoey Wallet, including prices, charts and screener information, may be sourced from third parties, may be delayed, and is provided for informational purposes only. It should not be relied upon as the sole basis for an investment decision.

03.09

Third-Party Services

Certain features of Zoey Wallet rely on third-party service providers, including our brokerage partner, identity-verification providers and market-data providers. Zoey Wallet is not responsible for the acts or omissions of these independent third parties.

03.10

No Investment Advice

Nothing on Zoey Wallet constitutes investment, legal or tax advice, or a recommendation or solicitation to buy or sell any security. You are solely responsible for your investment decisions, and you should consult a qualified professional where appropriate.

03.11

Execution and Order Risks

Orders placed through Zoey Wallet are subject to execution by our brokerage partner and are not guaranteed to be filled, or to be filled at a particular price, particularly during periods of high volatility, low liquidity, or system or market disruption.

03.12

Liquidity and Transfer Considerations

Some positions, particularly fractional shares, may be less liquid or more difficult to transfer than whole-share positions. You should review the transfer and liquidity terms applicable to your account before investing.

03.13

Technology and Operational Risks

As a technology platform, Zoey Wallet depends on the continued operation of its own systems, its brokerage partner's systems, and underlying network and blockchain infrastructure. Outages, interruptions or errors in any of these systems could affect your ability to access the platform or execute transactions.

03.14

Specific licensing, registration and jurisdictional information for Zoey Wallet and its brokerage partner will be published here following legal review, and this section should be treated as draft pending that review.

Additional Information

Additional risk disclosures specific to particular products or features may be presented to you at the relevant point in the Zoey Wallet application, and should be read in conjunction with this page.
