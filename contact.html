FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Zoey Wallet is a technology platform built to make access to global financial markets simpler for people across Africa. It's designed around a stablecoin-native model, funding and moving value using USDT and USDC, layered on top of access to U.S. equities and other global markets, rather than a traditional bank-account-first approach.

Zoey is built around giving you access to U.S. equities and other global markets through our technology platform, with investment transactions executed by a third-party regulated brokerage partner. Some features described on this site are still being rolled out. Where something isn't yet available in your account, the app will make that clear rather than leave you guessing.

A fractional share is a portion of a single share of stock, rather than the whole thing. Instead of needing enough money to buy one full share of an expensive company, fractional investing lets you put in whatever amount you have and own a proportional slice of that share.

Zoey is built around low-minimum, fractional investing: you can start investing in global equities for as little as $1, funded with supported stablecoins.

Zoey's funding model is stablecoin-native: the intended experience is to fund your account using supported stablecoins like USDT and USDC, which are then used to access investments on the platform. Deposit and withdrawal functionality is being built out in phases, and the app will always show you exactly what's available in your account at any given time.

Investments made through Zoey are held and executed through our third-party regulated brokerage partner, a FINRA/SIPC member, which is subject to the regulatory protections that apply to accounts held there. As with any investing, the value of your investments can go up or down, and past performance doesn't guarantee future results. See our Important Disclosures for more detail.

Zoey is built with an Africa-first focus, and much of our design, from stablecoin funding to fractional access, reflects that. That said, availability can depend on your specific country, eligibility and onboarding requirements, so not every country is automatically supported yet. If you're unsure whether Zoey is available where you are, reach out using the form on this page and we'll let you know.

Security is built into the platform at every layer: encrypted connections, secure authentication, controlled access to your account data, and ongoing monitoring for suspicious activity. We don't publish the specifics of our internal security architecture, for the same reason a bank doesn't publish its vault combination. Protecting your information and access to your account is a core design principle.
